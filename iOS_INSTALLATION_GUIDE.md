## 📱 How to Run or Install Xer0byte on iOS Devices

### Option 1: Instant Web & Streaming Mode (No Jailbreak / No Install Needed)
You can launch and test Xer0byte directly in **Safari** on your iPhone or iPad:
- **Direct Live Link:** [Launch Xer0byte Web Streaming](https://ais-pre-rwnp7qqnlqk43a6wrusrox-868695915078.asia-southeast1.run.app)
- **Home Screen App (PWA):**
  1. Open the link above in Safari on your iPhone.
  2. Tap the **Share** button (box with upward arrow) at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
  4. Now you have the **Xer0byte** app icon right on your iPhone home screen!

---

### Option 2: Sideloading onto iPhone via AltStore / Sideloadly
1. Download the **AltStore** or **Sideloadly** app on your PC or Mac.
2. Connect your iPhone to your computer via Lightning/USB-C cable.
3. Open Sideloadly, load the Xer0byte package, sign with your free Apple ID, and install onto your device.
4. On your iPhone, go to **Settings > General > VPN & Device Management** and trust the developer certificate.

---

### 🌐 Cross-Platform Bluetooth Note:
- Android devices use standard **Bluetooth RFCOMM (SPP)**.
- For iOS direct Bluetooth communication, iOS requires **CoreBluetooth BLE (Bluetooth Low Energy)** peripheral GATT services due to Apple's sandbox security policies.
- Xer0byte is actively extending the BLE peripheral advertiser to bridge Android & iOS offline peer-to-peer communication!
