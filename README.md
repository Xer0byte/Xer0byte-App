## 🚀 100% Offline — Zero Internet / Zero SIM Required

**Xer0byte** is designed for secure, private peer-to-peer communication within Bluetooth range (~100 meters):
- **Zero Mobile Data**: No internet, cellular plan, or Wi-Fi required.
- **Auto Peer Discovery**: Scans and bonds with nearby Bluetooth devices automatically.
- **🟢 Real-Time Online Green Dots**: Connected users immediately show glowing green online indicators.
- **Multi-Peer Simultaneous Mesh**: Multiple devices connect at the same time and chat seamlessly.
- **Authentic WhatsApp Interface**: 4 Tabs (**Chats**, **Updates/Status**, **Communities**, **Calls**), audio waveforms, 7-item attachment drawer, and Bluetooth calls.

## ⚡ How to Connect & Chat (Android Phone to Phone)

1. **Download & Install APK**:
   - Download the APK from the [Releases Link](../../releases/latest).
   - Install on 2 or more Android phones.
2. **Turn on Bluetooth**:
   - Turn on Bluetooth on all devices.
3. **Connect**:
   - On **Phone 1**: Open **Xer0byte**, tap the **Bluetooth icon** at the top, and tap **"Make Discoverable"**.
   - On **Phone 2**: Tap **"Scan Nearby"** and tap Phone 1 to connect.
4. **🟢 Green Dot Activated**:
   - Both devices will immediately show the bright green dot (`🟢`) and **ONLINE** badge!
5. Start chatting, sending voice notes, photos, documents, and testing peer audio calls.
